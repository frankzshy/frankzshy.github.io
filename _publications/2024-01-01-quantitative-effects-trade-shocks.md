This paper quantifies the effects of trade shocks under global supply chains, studying counterfactual scenarios in which the United States imposes additional tariffs on intermediate imports from China, with and without Chinese retaliation. The model features endogenous global sourcing, allowing the paper to isolate the role of supply chain reallocation in shaping the trade and welfare consequences of tariff shocks.

Presented at: 2024 Allied Social Science Associations (ASSA) Annual Meeting (Poster Session), 20th Annual GEP/CEPR Postgraduate Conference (Best Paper Prize, 2022), Empirical Investigations in International Trade (FREIT-EIIT), Midwest Trade and Theory Conference, 3rd QMUL Economics and Finance Workshop for PhD & Post-doctoral Students, 91st International Atlantic Economic European Conference.
