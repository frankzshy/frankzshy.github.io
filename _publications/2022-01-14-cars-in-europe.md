Using a multi-sector and multi-country general equilibrium model, this paper presents a quantitative assessment of the impact of global pandemic-induced labor supply shocks — both directly and via supply chains — during the initial phase of the COVID-19 pandemic on the auto sector and aggregate activity in Europe. The results show these labor supply shocks had a significant adverse impact on major auto producers in Europe, with about one-third of the decline in car-sector value added attributable to spillovers via supply chains within and across borders.
